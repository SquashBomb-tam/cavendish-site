Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026.

Who uses Cavendish

Cavendish is a personal assistant with a single user: its owner. It is not offered to anyone else, and it only connects to the owner's own Google account.

What Google data it can see

With the owner's permission, Cavendish has read-only access to:

  • the owner's Gmail (gmail.readonly), to find emails the owner asks about;
  • the owner's Google Drive (drive.readonly), to find files the owner asks about.

It cannot send, change or delete email or files.

How that data is used

Cavendish looks at Google data only to answer a request the owner has made. To write the answer, the relevant parts (for example an email's sender, subject and a short excerpt) are sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Nothing else is done with it.

  • It is not sold or shared with anyone for advertising.
  • It is not used to build or train AI models.
  • No person other than the owner reads it.

Cavendish's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is kept

Cavendish runs on the owner's own computer. The sign-in that lets it reach Google is stored only there. Google data is not copied to any other server, apart from what is sent to Anthropic to answer a request.

Removing access

The owner can cut Cavendish off from Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
