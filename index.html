Cavendish

A private assistant, used by one person.

Cavendish is a personal assistant that its owner runs on their own computer. It answers the owner's own questions by message, email and voice. It is not offered to the public, and there is nothing to sign up for.

When the owner allows it, Cavendish can search the owner's Gmail and Google Drive (read-only) to find an email or a file they ask about.
